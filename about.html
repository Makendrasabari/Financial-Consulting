Our Story

Founded on a Simple Conviction

Stackly Capital Partners was founded on the conviction that financial complexity shouldn't stand in the way of confident decisions. What began as a boutique advisory for growth-stage companies has become a trusted partner to CFOs, investors, and boards across 28 countries.

Today, our team combines institutional-grade financial rigor with the agility of a modern consultancy — advising on everything from capital structure to large-scale transformation.
